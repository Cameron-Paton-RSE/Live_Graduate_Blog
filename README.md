## 👋 About This Blog

This is more than a record — it's a growing knowledge base, a technical showcase, and an honest reflection of what it looks like to go from university into real-world control systems engineering at **[Ross-Shire Engineering (RSE)](https://www.ross-eng.com/)**.

Every project, every mistake, every breakthrough gets documented here.

> 🎓 BEng Electronics & Electrical Engineering — *University of Glasgow*  
> 🤖 MSc Mechatronics & Automation — *University of Strathclyde*  
> 🛠️ Graduate Control Systems Engineer — *Ross-Shire Engineering*  
> 📍 Based in Glasgow &nbsp;|&nbsp; 🍺 Former bartender &nbsp;|&nbsp; 🐀 Full-time rat enthusiast
